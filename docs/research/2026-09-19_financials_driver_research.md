## Current implementation and inherited evidence

The baseline `config/indicators.yaml` assigns all five financial tickers the same twelve indicators: `vix, usdjpy, eurjpy, dollar_index, nikkei225_futures, sp500_futures, nasdaq100_futures, us_2y_yield, us_10y_yield, us_30y_yield, xlf, kre`. The first ten come from `common`; XLF/KRE come from `financial`. MUFG and SMFG ADR definitions remain in the catalogue but have `enabled: false` and empty applicability. MFG/NMR/TKOMY are research symbols, not production indicator definitions. The initial local checkout was older; it must not be mistaken for this baseline.

`docs/research/2026-09-08-feature-reduction.json` records reduction, not demonstrated new OOS improvement: the reused 63-session/1,386-prediction study had MAE 1.2564 → 1.2512 percentage points, direction 0.5570 → 0.5584, rank IC 0.1119 → 0.1123. It did not pass its Bonferroni improvement test. Dropping ADRs changed only 14 predictions. Do not reinstate them solely because an economic rationale exists. The same record reports an 18-session live forecast-slope estimate of +0.072 (t=0.98), R²=0.0014, model MAE 1.337 percentage points versus 1.248 for zero return.

`config/indicator_candidates.yaml` and the 2026-08-17 research reject the Japanese bond/bank ETF group (2510.T/1615.T): rank IC 0.1119 → 0.0917; difference −0.0203, p=0.0172, 95% CI [−0.0368, −0.0037]. Four of five financials worsened. A further TOPIX addition also worsened MAE. These are historical experiment results, not new measurements. Exact redundancy of 10Y, 2Y and their spread is a reason to compare alternative parameterizations, not hold all three unrestricted.

## Per-ticker evidence and hypotheses

### 8306 Mitsubishi UFJ Financial Group

**Verified IR:** August 3 FY2026 Q1 highlights report net income ¥809.4bn (+48% YoY), with approximate +¥60bn net-income benefit from higher yen rates and +¥40bn from FX. Its business groups include domestic retail/commercial banking, Japanese and global CIB, global commercial banking, asset management/investor services and global markets. Krungsri/Bank Danamon and Morgan Stanley must not be treated as identical domestic bank exposures. The document distinguishes customer growth, market operations and equity-method earnings. [Q1 highlights, pp.2–6](https://www.mufg.jp/dam/ir/fs/2026/pdf/highlights2606_en.pdf).

The May presentation estimates an additional +25bp yen-rate shock at ¥100bn NII in year 1, ¥150bn in year 2 and ¥180bn in year 3, conditional on unchanged balance-sheet structure. This is a medium-term earnings sensitivity, **not** a daily stock-return coefficient. [FY2025 results presentation, p.50](https://www.mufg.jp/dam/ir/presentation/2025/pdf/speech2603_en.pdf). Precise current regional revenue/profit percentages and a per-¥1 FX sensitivity were not extracted and remain a gap.

**News hypothesis:** August 3 Reuters-attributed reporting describes stronger loan margins and record quarterly profit. Use this to prioritize yield and credit-risk hypotheses, not an earnings-news sentiment feature. [Dated report](https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2026/08/03/mufg-profit-jumps-48-as-loan-margins-improve/). MUFG identifies credit costs, funding liquidity, FX/rates/equities and its Morgan Stanley relationship as material risks. [Company risk disclosure](https://www.mufg.jp/dam/ir/report/annual_report/pdf/ar2025_en.pdf) is FY2025 and must not be represented as a newly reviewed FY2026 full risk inventory.

**Candidates:** MUFG ADR USD overnight return; USDJPY; XLF or JPM; US2Y/10Y changes, one yield-slope parameterization; MS return as a separate company-specific hypothesis; VIX. ADR/peer returns hypothesize positive co-movement; VIX negative risk sentiment; rates and FX have regime-dependent signs because margin gains compete with valuation losses and credit stress. US regional KRE is a stress proxy, not a direct business-composition match. JGB night futures need a licensed historical availability record. Commodity, gold, China or India factors are not admitted merely because the bank has international lending.

### 8316 Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group

**Verified IR:** July 31 FY3/27 Q1 materials distinguish Retail, Wholesale, Global and Global Markets. Global net business profit is ¥151.2bn; Global Markets ¥178.9bn. Global declined as loan origination was selective and the prior insurance settlement was absent; domestic deposit earnings/fees and markets improved. Asia multi-franchise net-income contribution is shown separately. These are management-accounting business results, not additive geographic revenue shares. [Q1 overview, pp.16–18](https://www.smfg.co.jp/english/investor/financial/latest_statement/2027_3/2027_1q_e03.pdf).

The May investor presentation estimates +25bp short/long yen rates adding ¥110bn NII in year 1 and ¥150bn in year 5. Its foreign-currency balance sheet is largely market-rate based; a +1% foreign-rate shift corresponds to about +¥20bn NII under the stated assumptions. The presentation also identifies Middle East stress, provisions, securities valuation and FX effects. [FY3/26 presentation, pp.6,10,22](https://www.smfg.co.jp/english/investor/financial/latest_statement/2026_3/2026_fy_e_pre.pdf). Current complete geographic profit percentages and per-¥1 FX sensitivity remain unverified.

**News hypothesis:** July 31 Reuters reports +33% Q1 profit with robust loans and a maintained ¥1.7tn annual forecast. This motivates bank margin/credit hypotheses without assuming rate increases always imply a positive next intraday return. [Reuters syndicated report](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/japan-s-smfg-posts-33-rise-in-first-quarter-profit-ce7f50dbdd88f324).

**Candidates:** SMFG ADR, USDJPY, XLF, KRE stress residual, US2Y/10Y change or curve, VIX; Jefferies only a separately justified peer candidate, not automatically selected from the strategic relationship. The official ADR ratio is 1 ADR : 0.6 common shares after October 1, 2024. Returns must handle corporate actions; a stale 0.2 ratio invalidates a price-parity feature. [ADR information](https://www.smfg.co.jp/investor/stock/adr.html), [ratio notice](https://www.smfg.co.jp/news_e/pdf/e20240515_05.pdf).

### 8411 Mizuho Financial Group

**Verified IR:** July 30 FY26 Q1 report: net income ¥422.9bn, FY outlook ¥1.4tn. Customer-group net business profits ¥335.8bn and Global Markets ¥199.7bn; customer subdivisions include retail/business, corporate/institutional, global CIB and asset management. Domestic loan/deposit margin is 1.26%. Non-JPY loans average USD252.7bn, with USD114.0bn Americas, USD89.4bn APAC and USD49.3bn EMEA. These are loan balances, not geographic profits. [Q1 results, pp.5–9,12](https://library.mizuhogroup.com/asset/9ff487db-e8c6-49e3-84e7-c94408d171ec/fg-data26_1q_2.pdf).

The May presentation estimates approximately +¥120bn annual pre-tax benefit per +25bp BOJ hike, subject to assumptions. It describes balance-sheet repricing, not a daily forecast sign. [Presentation, p.27](https://library.mizuhogroup.com/asset/8c340c4a-c9bb-4488-881f-db6a05acca02/fg_20260519_1.pdf). Current per-¥1 FX sensitivity and a fully reconciled regional-profit table remain gaps. Material research risks include credit deterioration, foreign funding and bond mark-to-market sensitivity; do not infer an unhedged exposure from gross loans.

**News hypothesis:** July 30 Reuters associates +45% Q1 profit and the higher forecast with firm loan demand despite Middle East energy/supply disruptions. This is reason to test margin and risk channels separately, not to add crude to every bank. [Reuters syndicated report](https://uk.marketscreener.com/news/mizuho-s-first-quarter-profit-rises-45-ce7f51d3d088f022).

**Candidates:** MFG ADR; USDJPY; XLF; US2Y/10Y/30Y selected sparsely; one curve term; VIX; a US CIB peer residual only in a subsequent bounded comparison. Official ADR is NYSE MFG, 5 ADRs : 1 common share. [Company stock information](https://www.mizuhogroup.com/investors/financial-information/stock-information). KRE and JPM should compete with XLF, not automatically multiply correlated bank features.

### 8604 Nomura Holdings

Q1 segment pretax profits are ¥71.1bn, ¥45.0bn, ¥93.3bn and ¥3.6bn respectively (total ¥213.0bn). Wholesale Global Markets revenue ¥318.7bn exceeds Investment Banking ¥50.4bn. Rising markets/client activity support wealth fees; investment management includes overseas assets. Therefore a domestic bank-style rate-only design misrepresents the company. [Company Q1 results explanation, July 30](https://plus.nomuraholdings.com/en/latest/20260729-FY202703-1Q-Highlights.html). Complete current regional revenue/profit shares and fixed FX/rate earnings sensitivities were not verified.

The company names market, credit, model, liquidity-linked capital constraints, operational and reputational risks; market risk includes rates, FX and securities. [Official risk framework](https://www.nomuraholdings.com/en/company/risk.html). July 29 Reuters identifies trading volatility and higher stock prices/wealth fees as drivers of a 39% profit increase. [Reuters syndicated report](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/nomura-s-first-quarter-profit-jumps-39-boosted-by-markets-ce7f51d2dc8cf620). Volatility can raise trading revenue while reducing risk appetite: VIX does not have an economically guaranteed negative daily coefficient.

**Candidates:** NMR ADR; GS/MS (one or a training-only residualized peer basket); SPY or S&P futures; QQQ only if incremental; VIX change/level competing alternatives; USDJPY; one or two US rate changes. XLF is a broad comparator. KRE is lower-priority and not an obligatory bank factor. ADR is NYSE NMR, 1 ADS : 1 ordinary share. [Official share data](https://www.nomuraholdings.com/jp/investor/shareholders/stock.html), [official quote symbol/delay](https://www.nomuraholdings.com/en/investor/stockprice/).
